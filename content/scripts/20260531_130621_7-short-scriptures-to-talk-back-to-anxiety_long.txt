Anxiety talks loud; talk back with these seven verses. If your thoughts feel like a storm you can’t turn off, let God’s words become the anchor you can hold in your hands and speak out loud in the moment you need them.

Anxiety is a storyteller. It imagines worst-case scenarios, amplifies what-ifs, and insists you’re alone. But Scripture gives us a different voice—clear, steady, and true. Today, I want to hand you seven short Scriptures you can memorize and use like a call-and-response. Anxiety talks. You talk back. And over time, you retrain your mind to run toward God’s voice first. I’ll also give you a simple seven-day plan to memorize these and a quick prayer you can use anytime panic rises.

Here’s your first verse, the anchor for everything else: Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God…and the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus. Philippians 4:6–7. When anxiety says, “You have to figure this out right now,” you answer, “I don’t carry this alone. I bring it to God with thanks, and His peace guards me.” Picture peace like a guard at the door of your mind. You can say it out loud: God, I’m handing this to you, and your peace is standing watch over me.
